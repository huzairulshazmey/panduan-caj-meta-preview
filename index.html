Apa itu Service Message?

What is a Service Message?

Mulai 1 Oktober, Meta mengenakan caj bagi Service Message — iaitu mesej balasan biasa yang dihantar oleh bisnes kepada customer. Ini ialah perubahan utama yang perlu diberi perhatian pengguna Wabot.

From 1 October, Meta charges for Service Messages — the ordinary reply messages a business sends to a customer. This is the main change Wabot users need to pay attention to.

Service Message ialah reply daripada flow chatbot, AI, bot atau ejen.

A Service Message is a reply from a chatbot flow, AI, bot or agent.

Contohnya: customer hantar keyword, flow membalas maklumat; AI menjawab pertanyaan; atau team membalas melalui Team Inbox. Setiap mesej balasan ini ialah Service Message.

For example: a customer sends a keyword and the flow replies with information; AI answers a question; or your team replies through Team Inbox. Every one of these reply messages is a Service Message.

1,000 mesej percuma setiap bulan bagi setiap nombor free messages every month for each number
Selepas itu After that Service Message seterusnya boleh dikenakan caj Meta mengikut kadar rujukan Malaysia. Further Service Messages may be charged by Meta at the Malaysia reference rate.

Yang tidak berubah: mesej daripada customer kepada bisnes tidak dikenakan caj Meta. Template Utility dan Marketing kekal mengikut kategori caj sedia ada. What does not change: messages from a customer to a business are not charged by Meta. Utility and Marketing templates remain under their existing charge categories.

Pengecualian penting untuk lead daripada Meta AdsImportant exception for leads from Meta Ads

72-hour Free Entry Point tidak dikenakan caj Meta

The 72-hour Free Entry Point is not charged by Meta

Jika lead masuk melalui Click-to-WhatsApp Ad atau Facebook Page CTA yang layak, dan bisnes membalas dalam tempoh yang melayakkan Free Entry Point, respons AI, bot atau ejen dalam tetingkap 72 jam ini tidak dikenakan caj Meta.

If a lead comes in through a qualifying Click-to-WhatsApp Ad or Facebook Page CTA, and the business replies within the window that qualifies for the Free Entry Point, AI, bot or agent responses inside this 72-hour window are not charged by Meta.

Jika lead senyap lebih 24 jam If the lead goes quiet for more than 24 hours

Free-form AI/bot reply tidak lagi dibenarkan. Follow-up perlu dihantar menggunakan approved template walaupun tempoh 72 jam masih aktif.

Free-form AI/bot replies are no longer allowed. Follow-ups must be sent using an approved template even while the 72-hour window is still active.

Soalan lazimFrequently asked questions

Jawapan ringkas sebelum anda buat semakan lanjut.

Quick answers before you look into it further.

Adakah semua reply WhatsApp akan dikenakan caj mulai 1 Oktober?Will every WhatsApp reply be charged from 1 October?

Tidak semestinya. Kesan caj bergantung pada jenis mesej, penggunaan WhatsApp Official API, keadaan perbualan dan sumber lead.

Not necessarily. The impact depends on the message type, your WhatsApp Official API usage, the state of the conversation and the lead source.

Adakah mesej daripada customer kepada bisnes dikenakan caj?Are messages from a customer to a business charged?

Tidak. Mesej inbound daripada customer kekal tidak dikenakan caj Meta.

No. Inbound messages from customers remain free of Meta charges.

Adakah ini kenaikan harga subscription Wabot?Is this a Wabot subscription price increase?

Tidak. Perubahan ini berkaitan caj Meta bagi WhatsApp Business Platform. Ia perlu dibezakan daripada yuran langganan Wabot, penggunaan AI dan caj pihak lain jika berkenaan.

No. This change relates to Meta charges for the WhatsApp Business Platform. It should be separated from your Wabot subscription fee, AI usage and any third-party charges.

Lead saya datang daripada Meta Ads. Adakah semua mesej percuma?My leads come from Meta Ads. Are all messages free?

Lead Click-to-WhatsApp yang layak boleh berada dalam 72-hour Free Entry Point. Namun, selepas customer senyap lebih 24 jam, bisnes masih perlu menggunakan approved template untuk follow-up.

Qualifying Click-to-WhatsApp leads can be inside the 72-hour Free Entry Point. However, once the customer has been quiet for more than 24 hours, the business still needs an approved template to follow up.

Bolehkah saya dapatkan kadar caj yang tepat?Can I get the exact charge rate?

Kadar boleh bergantung pada kategori mesej, negara nombor penerima, billing currency WhatsApp Business Account serta syarat Meta yang berkuat kuasa. Rujuk rate card Meta yang berkaitan untuk semakan terkini.

Rates can depend on the message category, the recipient number's country, the WhatsApp Business Account billing currency and Meta's terms in force. Refer to the relevant Meta rate card for the latest figures.
